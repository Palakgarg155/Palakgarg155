## 👩‍💻 About Me  
I’m an AI & ML engineer working across **Natural Language Processing, Generative AI, Data Science, and Computer Vision**.  
My work focuses on **building and evaluating reliable AI systems**—turning research ideas into solutions that work in the real world.

I’ve worked across the full AI development pipeline:  
- 🔍 Data curation & preprocessing  
- 🤖 Training and optimizing ML/DL models  
- 🔎 Benchmarking LLM-based pipelines  
- 🧩 Designing agentic & automated workflows  
- 🌐 Evaluating multilingual RAG architectures  
- 📉 Developing metrics for correctness, faithfulness & irrelevance filtering  

I love working on **Conversational AI**, AI safety, and evaluation systems that help measure model reliability, cost, latency, and guardrails.
